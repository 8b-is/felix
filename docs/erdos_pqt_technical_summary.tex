\section{Discrete Mathematics and Graph Percolation Verifiers}

In \texttt{crates/felix-lang/src/erdos\_pqt\_omniversal.rs} and \texttt{ada/erdos\_pqt\_spark.ads}, four classical discrete and analytic properties are evaluated and tested:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Geometrically Damped Ternary Stride Sum}: For a period-3 balanced-ternary sequence $\tau(n) \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ with $\tau(3m) = 0, \tau(3m+1) = +1, \tau(3m+2) = -1$, the dyadic-weighted homogeneous stride sum
  \[
    D(d) = \left| \sum_{k=1}^{K} \tau(k \cdot d) \, 2^{-k} \right| \le \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 2^{-k} = 1
  \]
  is bounded above by $1.0$ for all $d \in \mathbb{N}$ (evaluating to $0.285714$ across the first 16 primes and $0.0$ for $d \equiv 0 \pmod{3}$).
  \item \textbf{Erd\H{o}s--R\'enyi Percolation Threshold}: For $N = 24{,}917$ nodes, the critical edge probability for giant-component emergence is $p_c = \frac{\ln N}{N} \approx 0.0004063$.
  \item \textbf{Exhaustive Ramsey $R(3,3)=6$ Verification}: All $2^{\binom{6}{2}} = 2^{15} = 32{,}768$ two-colorings of $K_6$ are checked exhaustively to confirm the presence of at least one monochromatic triangle.
  \item \textbf{Erd\H{o}s--Kac Distinct Prime Factor Count $\omega(n)$ and Sunflower Intersection}: The function \texttt{distinct\_prime\_factors\_omega(n)} computes $\omega(n)$, while \texttt{Verify\_Sunflower\_Origo\_Kernel} proves in SPARK that $(A \wedge B) = Y \implies (A \wedge Y) = Y \wedge (B \wedge Y) = Y$.
\end{enumerate}
